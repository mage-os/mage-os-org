# Mage-OS General Assembly 2026 

# Looking Back and Moving Forward

The Mage-OS General Assembly 2026 was an opportunity to reflect on another year of growth, innovation, and community collaboration.

Over the past year, the project has continued to mature, delivering 8 releases, launching [Mage-OS 3.0](https://mage-os.org/releases/2026-05-18-mage-os-3-0-0-release), expanding its ecosystem of modules and tools, and strengthening its presence across Magento and eCommerce events around the world. What began several years ago as a safeguard for the Magento ecosystem has evolved into a thriving technology incubator focused on advancing the platform and supporting the community.

The technical achievements of the year were significant. Mage-OS 3.0 introduced an interactive installer, PHP 8.5 compatibility, a bundled [RMA module](https://mage-os.org/initiatives/2026-05-05-streamlining-returns-with-mage-os-rma), an Admin Activity Log, and the new Minimal Distribution initiative, which opens the door to more flexible and customizable Mage-OS deployments. Several community-driven projects also graduated from Mage-OS Labs into the core organization, demonstrating the strength of open collaboration and contribution.

The Assembly also reviewed the organization's financial position. Mage-OS remains financially healthy and stable, with a positive result for 2025 and reserves that allow the project to continue investing in development, release management, infrastructure, and community initiatives. At the same time, the Board acknowledged the importance of growing membership and securing sustainable funding to support the project's long-term ambitions.

A particularly important part of this year's Assembly was the scheduled Board rotation. This process was built into the organization's governance from the very beginning to ensure continuity while regularly welcoming new voices and perspectives.
